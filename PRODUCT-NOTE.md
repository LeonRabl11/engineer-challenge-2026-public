# Product note: the next two weeks

Pulse exists so a support team can see what needs attention and act on it
quickly. I would spend two weeks on that and cut what doesn't serve it.

## Build
1. **Make it safe to put in front of the client.** Move the AI call to the
   server and close the other critical and high issues. Non-negotiable
   before anything new.
2. **Faster triage in the inbox.** Filters for channel and owner next to the
   status filter, and changing the status with one click directly in the
   list – rebuilt properly, with feedback if the server request fails.
3. **A "needs my attention" view.** Items assigned to me, overdue and urgent
   first. That turns the old metrics numbers into a work queue.
4. **Keyword search in messages**, done safely, for questions like
   "who else complained about refunds?".

## Cut or park
- **CSV export:** risky (formula injection, token in the URL) and rarely the
  real need. Park it until the client says what they want to do with the data.
- **Customer health score:** it is unclear how it is calculated. A number
  nobody can explain creates distrust. Hide it until it is defined.
- **Decorative extras:** anything that doesn't help someone resolve feedback.
